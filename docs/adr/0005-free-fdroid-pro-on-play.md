# Free everywhere, one-time Pro unlock on Play only, and data is never limited

The app earns money through a one-time Pro unlock sold in the Play build. The F-Droid build has everything unlocked and shows a donate link instead. This is deliberate: don't "fix" it by locking features in the F-Droid build. F-Droid users rarely pay, they bring early adopters and bug reports, and DAVx⁵ and Simple Mobile Tools show this model works.

Pro only ever covers extras (QR Labels first, later things like quick capture, lending and themes). It never limits the number of Places or Items, and never locks photos, search, backup, restore or export behind it. Item limits, subscriptions and locked-away data are the most common complaints in competitors' reviews.

We rejected:
- **Paid upfront:** paid-upfront apps in this niche stay tiny.
- **Donations only:** too little income for the goal.
